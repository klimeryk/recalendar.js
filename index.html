ReCalendar

Your personalized calendar PDF for reMarkable and other e-ink tablets. Designed by you, generated in your browser.

ReCalendar is an open-source calendar generator. Tailor every detail to the way you plan, then download a ready-to-use PDF. No account, no upload, nothing ever leaves your device.

How it works

1

Customize it

Open the configurator and make it yours: layout, language, extra pages, habits and special dates.

2

Generate your PDF

Everything is rendered right in your browser. Your data stays with you, nothing is sent to a server.

3

Upload to your tablet

Built for planning

A few highlights

Optimized for e-ink

Uses the full screen and a stable layout to minimize refreshes. The PDF is a normal file, no hacks needed.

Navigate with links

Everything is cross-linked. Jump from month to week to day, with a mini calendar on every page.

Make it yours

A modern configurator lets you add pages, tweak itineraries and toggle sections. No code required.

Speaks your language

Robust internationalization: pick your first day of the week, first month and one of the built-in languages.
