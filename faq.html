The quick answers to what people ask most often.

It's a trade-off - all the work is done in your browser (your data is safe with you!), but the speed depends on your device. Make sure your browser is up-to-date and maybe close some tabs ;) You most likely will only need to generate the full calendar once a year, so I think it's a fair trade-off.

Alongside reMarkable tablets, ReCalendar now supports more e-ink devices (such as the Supernote A5 X) and lets you define a custom device with your own screen dimensions. If your device still isn't covered, please open a new issue on GitHub with the details and I'll try to add support for it.

Please check on GitHub if there's an open issue tracking your language. Upvote it to record your interest. Or better yet - help translate it!
